A useful notebook lets you return to an idea without having to reconstruct every step. A question, a sketch and a few lines about what you tried are often enough to get started.

Leave a trail you can follow

Write down the question before the calculation. Record what the symbols mean and where the assumptions enter. When something fails, keep a brief note about why.

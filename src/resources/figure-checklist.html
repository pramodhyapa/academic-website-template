Before sharing a figure, look at it at the size your reader will actually see.

Check the essentials

  • Label every axis and give units where they are needed.
  • Keep the meaning of colours consistent between related figures.
  • Use line styles or symbols as well as colour to distinguish data.
  • Write a caption that explains the main point.
  • Check that labels remain legible when the figure is reduced.

For interactive figures

Give controls clear labels and sensible initial values. Explain what changes when a reader moves a slider. Keep a useful static view for readers who prefer reduced motion.
